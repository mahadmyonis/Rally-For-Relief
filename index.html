What we do

Four ways we rally.

Sports tournaments

Competitive, welcoming tournaments where every entry goes toward the cause. Bring your best game — it's for something bigger.

Social events

Good food, good people, good energy — bringing the community together.

Community service

Rolling up our sleeves with local partners to get hands-on help where it's needed.

Fundraising campaigns

Campaigns that turn generosity into real relief. Every contribution, big or small, makes a difference.
